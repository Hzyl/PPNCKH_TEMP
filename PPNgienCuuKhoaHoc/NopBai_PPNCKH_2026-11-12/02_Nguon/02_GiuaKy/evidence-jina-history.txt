# Journal History

2016 The inaugural issue was released. _Data_ was published as a quarterly journal.
2017 _Data_ was indexed in [Emerging Sources Citation Index (ESCI)—Web of Science (Clarivate Analytics)](https://jcr.clarivate.com/jcr-jp/journal-profile?app=jcr&referrer=target%3Dhttps:%2F%2Fjcr.clarivate.com%2Fjcr-jp%2Fjournal-profile%3Fjournal%3DDATA-BASEL%26year%3D2023%26fromPage%3D%252Fjcr%252Fhome&Init=Yes&journal=DATA-BASEL&authCode=null&year=2023&fromPage=%2Fjcr%2Fhome&SrcApp=IC2LS), starting with Volume 2 (Issue 1).
2018 _Data_ was indexed in Inspec - IET.  
_Data_ became an affiliated journal of the[Global Open Data for Agriculture and Nutrition](https://www.godan.info/) (GODAN).
2019 _Data_ was indexed in [Scopus (Elsevier)](https://www.scopus.com/sourceid/21100924372), starting with Volume 1 (Issue 1).
2020 _Data_ received its first CiteScore (2019) of 2.1.  
_Data_ established the following sections:

*   _Chemoinformatics;_
*   _Computational Biology, Bioinformatics, and Biomedical Data Science;_
*   _Information Systems and Data Management;_
*   _Spatial Data Science and Digital Earth;_
*   _Featured Reviews of Data Science Research_.
2021 _Data_ adopted a monthly publication schedule.
2023 _Data_ received its first Impact Factor (2.6).
2024 _Data_ was indexed in Ei Compendex—[Elsevier](https://www.engineeringvillage.com/home.url).
2026 The year 2026 marked the [10th Anniversary](https://www.mdpi.com/journal/data/anniversary) of _Data_.  
Section "_Chemoinformatics_" was renamed as "_Data Science for Chemistry, Energy and Materials_".  
Section "_Spatial Data Science and Digital Earth_" was renamed as "_Spatial Data Science for Environment and Earth_".
